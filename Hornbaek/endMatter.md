# Wrap-up {.r-fit-text}

- **Perception** is constructed from sensory information, expectation, and attention; spend your saliency budget and fight clutter.
- **Fitts' Law** makes the speed-accuracy trade-off quantitative: big and close is fast, small and far is slow.
- **Memory** is reconstruction, not storage: design for recognition, not recall, and never lean on working memory.
- **Mental models** and **heuristics** are how people cope with opaque systems, including AI: make behavior legible and predictable.
